AquaGuard Plus targets the bacterial pathogens most commonly responsible for fin rot, ulcer disease and bacterial gill infection in farmed freshwater fish. Its fast-absorbing formula begins working within hours of application, reducing further tissue damage and stopping the spread of infection across the pond.

  • Usage: Mix 1ml per 10 litres of pond water. Apply in the early morning. Repeat every alternate day for 3 doses.
  • Storage: Store below 30°C in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.
  • Compatibility: Suitable for all freshwater fish species including tilapia, carp and catfish.
  • Controls bacterial infection within 48 hours
  • Improves gill health and oxygen uptake
  • Safe for all freshwater species
  • No known resistance reported in field studies
