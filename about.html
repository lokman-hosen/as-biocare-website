Our Story

Founded on a simple belief: healthy fishes build healthy livelihoods

AS BIO CARE LIMITED was established to close the gap between advanced veterinary science and everyday farmers. What began as a small formulation unit has grown into a full-scale manufacturing operation supplying fisheries medicines, poultry healthcare products, water treatment solutions and feed supplements to farms across Bangladesh.

Today, our products reach more than 50,000 farmers through a network of 35+ depots and 450+ dealers, supported by a dedicated advisory and research team.

"When we started AS Bio Care, our goal was never just to sell medicine — it was to give every farmer the confidence that their hard work would not be lost to preventable disease. That commitment still guides every decision we make."

Dr. Aminul Islam
Chairman

"Our expansion across 35 depots wasn't about scale for its own sake. It was about making sure no farmer, however remote, has to wait for the treatment their farm needs."

Shirin Akter
Managing Director
