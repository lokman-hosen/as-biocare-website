About Our Company

Seventeen years of dedicated fish healthcare innovation

Since our founding, AS BIO CARE LIMITED has grown into one of the most trusted names in aquaculture and poultry healthcare across Bangladesh. Our in-house research team develops fisheries medicines, nutritional supplements, water treatment solutions and disease-prevention formulas that keep farms productive and animals healthy.

Every product that leaves our facility is tested against strict quality standards before it reaches a single depot — because a farmer's livelihood depends on it working the first time.
